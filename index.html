RE:MEMBER · working prototype

Before you start

This page places a representation of someone you love into your room through your phone camera. It is a tool for remembrance, not a way to bring a person back.

Everything stays on this device. This page does not upload your files to any server. (The optional background-removal step downloads an AI model from a public CDN, but your photo is processed in your browser.)

Add source material

How should the person appear?

If the person is seated in the image, use their seated height.

Ready

  1. Stand in the room where the memory belongs, with good light.
  2. Move the phone slowly until a ring appears on the floor or sofa.
  3. Tap to place. Use the buttons to adjust height, size and direction.
